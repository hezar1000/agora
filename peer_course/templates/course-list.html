    Please Read the Terms Below

    Welcome! Before you start using Agora, please review these guidelines carefully. If you have any questions, contact agora.help@ubc.ca for clarifications. Understanding these guidelines helps you know what to expect from Agora and what is expected from you.

    Your Personal Information

    When you use Agora:

    • Be cautious about what you share: Avoid sharing sensitive information that is not required for classroom activities. Additionally, refrain from disclosing any information with which you are uncomfortable sharing with the rest of the class.
    • Photo Use: You can choose not to have your photo displayed during classroom activities. To do this, go to your profile settings in Agora and delete your photo.
    • Need help opting out? If you are having trouble or need help, please reach out to agora.help@ubc.ca.
    What We Collect and Why
    • Participation, messages, and poll responses during class: to enhance classroom discussions and interactions.
    • Images for identification (optional): If you are comfortable, you can upload your photo to help instructors recognize you during classes.

    All this information is used to support your learning and participation in class. We keep this information only as long as needed to serve its educational purpose, following UBC’s guidelines for retention.

    Follow the Rules

    By using Agora, you agree to follow the rules outlined here and other UBC policies, such as the Academic Integrity and Student Code of Conduct. These rules help ensure that everyone uses the system fairly and respectfully.
