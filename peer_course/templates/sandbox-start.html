What happens when you press it

  1. A class appears with a lecture already running, so hands can go up straight away.
  2. You get a six-digit code. Anyone who opens the join page and types it is in, with no account — a second browser of your own, or a room full of people.
  3. Raised hands appear on your screen, and you can call on one at random, exactly as you would in your own course.
  4. It deletes itself, along with everyone who joined it.
